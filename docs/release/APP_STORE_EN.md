## Promotional text

Collect hearts in a mechanical storybook garden, dodge four big egos, and turn
the chase around with Power Hearts. A satirical first-person arcade adventure.

## Description

Welcome to Maze of Egos. Play as Zelenskyy and collect hearts between colorful
garden walls, mechanical shortcuts, and four pursuers with their own tactics.

Collect all 244 hearts to reach the next level. Power Hearts briefly turn the
chase around, while banana peels, oil slicks, and speed boosts help you escape
tight situations.

• Move automatically along the center of each corridor. Use the stick to choose your next turn.
• Face four different pursuers and increasing difficulty.
• Watch for moving walls and clearly signaled shortcuts.
• Save your best score and pause or continue a local game.
• Adjust sound, music, haptics, field of view, and reduced motion.
• Use a left-handed layout or a lower graphics setting.
• Play offline, with no account, ads, or in-app purchases.

Fictional political satire, unaffiliated with the people depicted or any
established game franchise. Harmless cartoon comedy with no weapons or injury.

## App Review notes

No login or server connection is required. Tap PLAY. Your character moves
automatically: stick left/right queues the next available turn, up selects
straight ahead, and down turns around. Release the stick to keep moving.
The camera follows horizontally. The item button becomes available after you
collect a question mark. Collect all 244 hearts to finish a level.

Pause opens settings and the main menu. CONTINUE SAVED GAME restores local
progress in a paused state. The game uses English on every device language.
Sound and music honor the in-game settings and media volume, and can play when
the iPhone Ring/Silent switch is set to silent.

This is political satire. No recordings of the depicted people's voices are
used. Support opens the system browser. Gameplay works offline.
