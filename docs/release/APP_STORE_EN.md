## Promotional text

Choose your ego, collect hearts and outsmart four computer rivals in a 3D garden maze. Two-minute rounds, playful tricks and a race to three trophies.

## Description

Five egos enter a mechanical garden. Who will take home the trophies?

Tap a character card — its picture or name — to choose Kim, Putin, Trump, Xi or Zelenskyy. Every character has the same abilities.
Compete against four computer players, collect hearts and find playful surprises
inside question boxes. A close overhead 3D camera shows the corridors around you.

Each heart earns one point. The round ends when every heart is collected or after two minutes. The highest score wins a trophy;
tied leaders share the round victory. The first player to three trophies wins
the match. Total points break a tie, and equal totals share the final victory.

• Move automatically along the middle of each corridor. Point toward a corridor on screen to queue your next turn.
• Slip up rivals with bananas, slow them with oil or speed ahead with a boost.
• Aim water balloons and cream pies along your heading. Watch the blue attack warnings and change your route.
• Return to the centre after a five-second knockout, keeping every point. Three seconds of protection help you get moving again.
• Start each round in a random corridor. Collected hearts stay collected until the next round.
• Plan your route with a minimap showing remaining hearts and every active player.
• See round gold, silver and bronze with character portraits, names and points, then track golden trophies in the overall standings.
• Discover refilling question boxes and automatic mechanical shortcuts.
• Pause, save and continue a local match, including its scores and trophies.
• Adjust sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, graphics and the left-handed control layout.
• Play offline, with no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

Fictional political satire, unaffiliated with the people depicted or any
established game franchise. Harmless cartoon comedy without injury graphics.

## App Review notes

No login or server connection is required. Tap PLAY, then choose a character.
One human competes against four local computer players; online multiplayer is
not included. All characters use identical abilities and movement rules.

Point the stick toward a corridor on screen. Movement is automatic, turns are
remembered and releasing the stick keeps the character moving. Walls stop you.
The overhead camera follows the player without rotating. Collect a question
box, then tap the item button. The next box replaces the held item.

Players begin each round at separate random locations. Collected hearts do not respawn during that round. A minimap shows remaining hearts and active players. Gold, silver and bronze reflect round points; only gold earns an overall trophy. Overall standings appear below the round medals.

Rounds end when every heart is collected, or at two minutes of active gameplay. Collect the most hearts to win a
trophy. The match ends when a player earns three trophies. Shared victories
are supported. Contact with another player is harmless. Attacks use short,
wall-blocked paths and visible warnings. Knockouts keep all points, wait five
seconds, then return the player to the centre with temporary protection.

Pause opens settings and help. CONTINUE SAVED GAME restores the competition
in a paused state. Settings and progress are stored only on the device. The
game uses English on every device language. Sound and music honor in-game
settings and media volume, and can play with the iPhone Silent switch on.

No recordings of the depicted people's voices are used. Support opens the
system browser. Gameplay works offline.
