# Maze of Egos — privacy policy draft

Date: October 21, 2026. Applies to version 0.2.0, build 12.
Draft for publication at a stable public URL before App Store submission.

Maze of Egos is an offline arcade game. It does not require an account and has
no ads, in-app purchases, analytics tools, or third-party logins. The game does
not send gameplay, scores, identifiers, or other player information to a server.

Your best score, settings, and current game are stored locally in the app's
storage. They stay on your device; normal device backups may include them.
Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. Existing device
backups are managed through Apple's backup settings.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or tracking
identifiers. Haptics, sound, and music can be turned off. Diagnostics collected
by Apple through your device settings are managed by Apple.

The Support button opens your browser at
https://donkeytrump-api-p.azurewebsites.net/support. When you visit a website,
its host may process technical connection information such as your IP address.
If you choose to send a support request, the information you provide is handled
through the contact channel you use. Do not include sensitive information in
public issues.

Contact and responsible person: Jesper Bjerre, through the support page above.

## Publication status
